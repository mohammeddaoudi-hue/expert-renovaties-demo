How your renovation works

Four steps. You know in advance what happens and when.

  1. 01

    Site visit

    You show us the space and tell us what you want from it. We measure up and tell you what is technically possible.

  2. 02

    Quote

    Broken down item by item, with the materials listed, so you can see what you are paying for.

    The work

    The same team does the structural work and the finishing. We screen off the work area and keep the rest of your home clean.
